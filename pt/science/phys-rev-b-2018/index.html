Optical orientation and alignment of excitons in ensembles of inorganic perovskite nanocrystals

M. O. Nestoklon, S. V. Goupalov, R. I. Dzhioev, O. S. Ken, V. L. Korenev, Yu. G. Kusrayev, V. F. Sapega, C. de Weerd, L. Gomez, T. Gregorkiewicz, Junhao Lin, Kazutomo Suenaga, Yasufumi Fujiwara, L. B. Matyushkin, I. N. Yassievich

Physical Review B, 97 (23), 235304, 2018

Resumo

We demonstrate the optical orientation and alignment of excitons in a two-dimensional layer of CsPbI₃ perovskite nanocrystals prepared by colloidal synthesis and measure the anisotropic exchange splitting of exciton levels in the nanocrystals. From the experimental data at low temperature (2 K), we obtain the average value of anisotropic splitting of bright exciton states of the order of 120 μeV. Our calculations demonstrate that there is a significant contribution to the splitting due to the nanocrystal shape anisotropy for all inorganic perovskite nanocrystrals.
