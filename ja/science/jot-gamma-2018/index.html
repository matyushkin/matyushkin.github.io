Effect of gamma irradiation on the photoluminescence of CsPbBr₃ and CdSe/ZnS nanocrystals

L. B. Matyushkin, N. M. Romanov

Journal of Optical Technology, 85 (2), 119–121, 2018

要旨

The influence of gamma radiation of a ¹³⁷Cs source on the photoluminescence of perovskite CsPbBr₃ nanocrystals is reported in this study. Stability of photoluminescence in perovskite CsPbBr₃ nanocrystals is compared with commercial samples of CdSe/ZnS nanocrystals. The results suggest the possibility of using CsPbBr₃ nanocrystals as biomarkers in order to improve antitumor therapy.
