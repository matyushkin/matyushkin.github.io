# matyushkin.github.io

The personal site of Leo Matyushkin and the tools it offers its readers. This
glossary names the concepts of those tools.

## Typing trainer

**Course**:
Everything needed to learn touch typing in one language: its keyboard layout,
its letters in the order they open, and its word list.
_Avoid_: lesson, language pack

**Letter**:
One unit of the course that opens and is measured on its own. A final form
is a letter of its own: ם sits on another key than מ, and is typed at
another speed.
_Avoid_: key, character

**Open letter**:
A letter the generated text may contain. A course starts with its six most
frequent letters open.
_Avoid_: unlocked, active, learned

**Target speed**:
The speed every open letter must reach before the next letter opens, in
words per minute (five keystrokes make a word). The reader may change it.
_Avoid_: goal, threshold

**Ready letter**:
An open letter typed at the target speed or faster with at most 5% errors.
When every open letter is ready, the next letter opens.
_Avoid_: mastered, learned

**New letter**:
The letter that opened most recently, until it is ready.

**Weak letter**:
An open letter that is not ready. The weakest one comes up most often in the
text.
_Avoid_: problem letter, slow letter

**Practice time**:
The time spent typing in the trainer, today and in all: the gaps between
keystrokes, a pause longer than three seconds not counted. Resetting the
letters keeps it.
_Avoid_: session length, time on page
