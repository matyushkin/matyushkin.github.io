Growth characteristics of nanostructured lead sulfide films prepared by chemical bath deposition

P. K. Afonicheva, L. B. Matyushkin

IEEE EIConRusNW 2016, pp. 9–11

초록

Processes of lead sulfide film forming from aqueous solutions are considered. Two main stages of such film growth are highlighted. It is shown that the first stage can be used to produce arrays of lead sulfide quantum dots.
